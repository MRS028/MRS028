### 🔬 How I Approach an ML Experiment

A model's output matters, but so does the process that produced it. My goal is to develop a workflow that others can follow and evaluate:

1. **Understand the problem:** Identify what the prediction is supposed to achieve and why it matters.
2. **Inspect the data:** Check quality, class distribution, missing values, and potential leakage.
3. **Set up evaluation:** Build reasonable train/validation/test splits and choose appropriate metrics.
4. **Start with a baseline:** Compare sophisticated models against something simple and understandable.
5. **Experiment carefully:** Change one important factor at a time when possible, and record results.
6. **Analyze limitations:** Look for failure modes, overfitting, instability, and misleading metrics.
7. **Document findings:** Make the code, configurations, and outcomes easier to reproduce.

I see research as a process of asking better questions—not simply collecting impressive-looking results.
