### 🔬 How I Approach an ML Experiment

1. **Understand the problem:** what the prediction should achieve and why it matters.
2. **Inspect the data:** quality, class balance, missing values and potential leakage.
3. **Set up evaluation:** sound splits and metrics that fit the problem.
4. **Start with a baseline:** compare complex models against something simple.
5. **Experiment carefully:** change one important factor at a time and record results.
6. **Analyze limitations:** failure modes, overfitting, instability and misleading metrics.
7. **Document findings:** make code, configurations and outcomes reproducible.

I see research as learning to ask better questions, not collecting impressive-looking numbers.
